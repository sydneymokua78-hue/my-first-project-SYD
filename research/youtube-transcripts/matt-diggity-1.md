\## Chapter 2: AI Bot Manager



We want to build an AI system that can handle hundreds of SEO tasks automatically.



We use Make.com, a no-code automation tool connected to ChatGPT.



This allows us to generate content, metadata, and even link-building campaigns at scale.



The idea is topical authority — the more quality content you have on a topic, the more Google trusts your site.



You extract keyword opportunities using tools like Ahrefs, then feed them into a spreadsheet.



That spreadsheet becomes input for the AI system.



\---



\## Chapter 3: Connecting Platforms



You connect Google Sheets to Make.com.



Then connect OpenAI (ChatGPT API) to the workflow.



This allows automation of content generation.



You create prompts that first generate outlines, then full articles based on those outlines.



This two-step process improves output quality significantly.



\---



\## Chapter 4: Generating Outline



You instruct AI to generate structured outlines based on keywords.



Then you feed that outline into a second prompt to generate full content.



This ensures consistency, structure, and SEO optimization.



You can scale this to hundreds or thousands of pages.



\---



\## Chapter 5: Checking the Results



The system outputs ready-to-publish articles into Google Docs.



The content is not perfect, but it is scalable and effective for topical authority.



This allows automation of low-value content while humans focus on high-value pages.



Using this system, the client’s revenue grew significantly.



From $166,000 to $491,000 per month, with 266% traffic growth.
