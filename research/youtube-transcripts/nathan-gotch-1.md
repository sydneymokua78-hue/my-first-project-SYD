\# Nathan Gotch - How to Create SEO Content That Actually Ranks in 2026

\## Step 1: Pick a keyword you can compete on



To rank SEO content in Google and AI platforms like ChatGPT, start by choosing a keyword you can actually compete for.



Look for low authority competitors (even DR0 sites ranking in top positions). This shows you can outrank them with some authority.



Use tools like Ahrefs and apply low DR filters to find weak competition keywords.



\---



\## Step 2: Create a knowledge base



Create a ChatGPT project that acts as your SEO brain.



Include:

\- Business info (name, website, contacts)

\- Services/products

\- Unique selling points

\- Testimonials and reviews

\- Policies and guarantees



Upload this to ChatGPT Projects so the AI can generate grounded, accurate content.



You can also upload:

\- Sales calls

\- Surveys

\- Internal documents



This prevents hallucinations and improves accuracy.



\---



\## Step 3: Build SEO content research



Create a Google Sheet with competitors.



Collect top 10 competitors using Google and scraping tools.



Use AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode to expand research.



Use prompt generators to turn keywords into natural language queries.



Export citations and add them to your sheet.



\---



\## Step 4: Create SEO outline



Upload competitor research into ChatGPT Project.



Use AI to generate an SEO-optimized outline based on research.



This ensures structured, relevant, and intent-matching content.



\---



\## Step 5: Create the first draft



Use the outline to generate a full article with AI.



Then review with a human to verify facts and quality.



Only publish once it passes quality control.



\---



\## Step 6: Page design for conversions



Design pages for conversions, not just content.



For commercial pages:

\- Focus on structure

\- Improve user experience

\- Build conversion-focused layout



Send wireframe to designer if needed.



\---



\## Post-publishing strategy



After publishing, SEO work continues:



\### 1. Track performance

Use AI SEO tools to track rankings across Google and AI platforms.



\### 2. Internal linking

Use Screaming Frog to find internal linking opportunities.



\### 3. Expand topical coverage

Create more related pages to strengthen authority.



\### 4. Build backlinks

\- Analyze competitor backlinks

\- Use HARO / Featured.com

\- Do expert interviews

\- Use AI for link prospecting



\---



\## Final takeaway



Modern SEO is a system:

\- Research → AI → Content → Design → Links → Expansion



Success comes from combining AI tools with strong SEO fundamentals.
