Okay enough bragging, let's get into the actual steps. And don't worry, this isn't going to be the same generic SEO advice you've probably seen a thousand times before like great content, use keywords on your page, make sure your site loads quickly and all that stuff. That stuff can help a little bit and this is an SEO video for beginners so it is important to get the basics right. But if you want to rank for competitive keywords that stuff might help a little bit but it's not really going to take your site to the first page of Google for really competitive keywords.



What will are the steps that I'm about to lay out for you right now starting with step number one which is to find five keywords. So you probably already know that SEO is really all about keywords it's about ranking for keywords that your customers search for and there are a bunch of different strategies tools and tactics for finding them but one of the easiest and most effective is to use Google autocomplete. All you need to do for this to work is type in a keyword that you think a customer might search for and look for the suggestions that Google gives you.



These are great for two reasons. First of all these keywords are usually keywords people are searching for now so they tend to be trending a little bit that way you can get in early and sort of ride the wave as more people search for that term. But more importantly they are usually long tail keywords which are much less competitive than most keywords that people go after.



One of the biggest mistakes people make that are new to SEO is that they go after really really competitive keywords and they are usually keywords that people first think of when they think of their business. So for example let's say you run a skincare brand and you want to create a blog post around the keyword skincare routine. Now on the surface this is a good keyword because it's something that your customers probably search for. The problem is it's just way too competitive for most new sites to rank for. You have huge brands trying to rank for this keyword.



A long tail version of this keyword would be something like skincare routine for men or skincare routine for dry skin. These two keywords are much less competitive than skincare routine so even if your site is new you still have a chance to rank for them.



So once you're done with Google autocomplete scroll down to the bottom of Google to the related searches section. Here is where Google will show you keywords that are directly related to your keyword and sometimes more tangentially related so it's almost like brainstorming within Google.



And speaking of keywords that are low competition a great way to find them is a tool called Exploding Topics. This leads us to the second step which is to create amazing content out of each of the five keywords that you found.



At this point you should have found at least five keywords that are a good fit for your business and also relatively low competition. Now it's time to create amazing content around those keywords.



Back in the day amazing content meant huge guides that covered everything. But in SEO today the big thing is search intent. What that means is when someone searches for something there is a reason behind it. Sometimes they are looking for information, sometimes to buy something, and sometimes to compare products.



Each keyword has a unique search intent and the better your content satisfies that intent the higher you will rank in Google.
