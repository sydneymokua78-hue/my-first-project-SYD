[Cool new update from Discover Snoop: Individual LinkedIn Posts are now appearing in Google Discover. 

This is in addition to X, Instagram, and TikTok posts already showing more frequently in there. And as Damien Andell has recently been highlighting, this appears to work a lot better for individuals/entities with an established presence in Google's Knowledge Graph and who have a big social following. 

Damien even recently asked Discover for more topics from certain creators, and immediately saw more of their social posts in his Discover feed.

I see this as an evolution of the E-E-A-T work Google has been doing for years to both highlight individual experts and creators, as well as to promote authentic, human content. 🤔 Not just in Search, but lately much more in Google Discover too.
