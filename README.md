# AI-Powered SEO Content Production Research

## Overview
This project explores how leading SEO and content marketing experts use AI, automation, and repeatable systems to scale content production. The goal is to collect high-signal insights and turn them into a practical playbook for real-world execution.

## Why This Topic
AI-powered SEO is reshaping how content is planned, created, optimized, and distributed. This research focuses on how top practitioners combine strategy with tooling to drive sustainable organic growth.

## What Was Collected
- 10 carefully selected experts in SEO and content marketing
- YouTube transcripts with in-depth explanations and tactical detail
- LinkedIn posts with timely insights and execution-focused thinking

## Repository Structure
- `sources.md` - Overview of selected experts and selection rationale
- `research/linkedin-posts/` - Collected LinkedIn posts organized by expert
- `research/youtube-transcripts/` - Full transcripts of selected videos
- `research/other/` - Additional materials (optional)

## Expert Selection Criteria
Experts were selected based on:
- Proven experience in SEO, SaaS, or content marketing
- Consistent publishing of actionable insights
- Focus on AI, automation, or scalable content systems
- Strong reputation in the SEO and marketing industry

## Key Insights
- AI is primarily used to scale content production, not replace strategy
- High-performing SEO depends on systems and frameworks, not isolated tactics
- Content distribution is as important as content creation
- Programmatic SEO and automation workflows are recurring themes
- Authority and trust signals remain critical for rankings
- Consistent publishing and iterative testing outperform assumptions

## Next Steps
- Synthesize findings into a structured SEO playbook
- Identify repeatable frameworks across experts
- Test selected strategies in real-world scenarios

## Conclusion
This research provides a strong foundation for understanding how modern SEO content is produced at scale using AI-assisted workflows and strategic systems.
